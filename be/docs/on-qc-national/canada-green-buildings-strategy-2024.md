# Canada Green Buildings Strategy (CGBS) 2024

**Launched:** July 16, 2024  
**Department:** Natural Resources Canada  
**Goal:** Transform Canada's building sector for a net-zero and resilient future by 2050

## Overview

The Canada Green Buildings Strategy (CGBS) is the Government of Canada's plan to enhance energy efficiency in homes and buildings, reduce energy costs for Canadians, and create jobs nationwide. Buildings are the third-largest source of greenhouse gas emissions in Canada.

## Key Targets

- **3% annual retrofit rate** for existing buildings
- **3.5 million new net-zero homes** by 2030
- **37% reduction** in building sector emissions below 2005 levels by 2030
- **Net-zero emissions** by 2050
- **Phase out oil heating systems** in new constructions by 2028

## Key Initiatives

### 1. Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program (CGHAP)
- **$800 million** investment
- Assists low-to-median-income Canadians, including tenants
- Free retrofits managed by third parties (direct install model)
- Replaces the previous Canada Greener Homes Grant
- Offers up to 4x the value of the former grant

### 2. Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program
- Helps households transition from oil heating to electric heat pumps
- Significant annual savings for households currently using oil heating

### 3. National Approach to Home Labelling
- Developing a national framework for assessing and labelling home energy performance

### 4. Modernizing the Energy Efficiency Act
- Updating standards to incorporate new energy-saving technologies
- Promoting heat pumps to replace traditional air conditioners in new homes

### 5. Buy Clean / Low-Carbon Materials
- "Buy Clean" measures for low-carbon materials in public infrastructure
- Encouraging use of clean and embodied carbon in construction through federal investments

### 6. Building Codes and Standards
- Updates to critical building programming to support net-zero code development
- Codes Acceleration Fund to accelerate code adoption, compliance and enforcement

### 7. Climate Resilience
- Making buildings safer and more comfortable amid changing climate conditions
- Federal government committing to clean energy for federal buildings

## Progress to Date

- **240,000 homeowners** already helped through previous grant program
- **Average savings:** Nearly $400 annually on energy bills
- **280,000+ heat pumps** installed since 2020

## Investment Required

Estimated **$20-$32 billion annually** needed for retrofits to meet climate goals.
Over **$400 billion** investment required over 30 years for large-scale retrofits.

## Challenges Addressed

1. Housing affordability
2. Energy efficiency
3. Climate change and resilience
4. Slow pace of retrofitting buildings
5. Labor shortages in construction trades (projected need: 500,000 additional workers)
